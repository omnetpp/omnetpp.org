Following the release of OMNeT++ 6.5, the core development team is hosting an
online community Q&A and catch-up on Google Meet. We will give a brief overview
of what shipped in 6.5 and what we are working on for upcoming releases, followed
by an informal open discussion about your projects, custom models, feedback, or
any technical questions you have for the team.

**Date:** Wednesday, November 11, 2026<br>
**Time:** 14:00 – 15:00 CET<br>
**Google Meet:** [https://meet.google.com/ggw-uiif-jof](https://meet.google.com/ggw-uiif-jof)

Whether you want to showcase what you are building, ask about the new features,
or just meet the team and fellow developers, all are welcome. Please signal your
intent to join on the [discussion page](https://github.com/orgs/omnetpp/discussions/1533).
